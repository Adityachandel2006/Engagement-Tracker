# Labelling Rules

1. Judge the feeling toward the PRODUCT as a customer would, not toward the company's stock or earnings.
2. A calm report of a defect (sole detached, seam split) is negative.
3. Specs, patents, restock and store-opening announcements are neutral.
4. If the text is mixed, choose the dominant feeling.
5. If the text is not really about the product, set human_relevant to 0.
Allowed values: human_relevant is 1 or 0; human_sentiment is positive, negative or neutral.
